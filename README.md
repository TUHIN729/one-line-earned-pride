## The entry

One line, written by `main.ts` to `/output/entry.txt`:

> The old carpenter ran his thumb along the dovetail he'd cut three times, and this time it closed without a gap.

A quiet, earned moment of pride: an old carpenter, three failed cuts behind him, and the joint finally closing without a gap. The effort is felt inside the line ("cut three times"); the pride arrives silently in the last words, never asserted or congratulated. No trophy, no podium, no graduation — just a thumb, a dovetail, and a gap that is finally gone.
